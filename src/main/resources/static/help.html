How does the food scan work?

Upload a photo. The app processes it, detects the food, calculates nutrition for one serving and gives tips for your goal. Typing the food name in the scan box improves accuracy.

Are the calories exact?

They are estimates for a typical serving. Your actual portion may differ, so treat them as a guide.

How do I change my daily targets?

Open Goals from the menu, pick your goal and adjust calories, protein and water.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. Delete single scans from History or remove your whole account in Settings.
